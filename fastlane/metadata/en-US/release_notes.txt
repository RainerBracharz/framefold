Sharpness: FrameFold now measures image sharpness continuously. Focus is only locked once it has actually settled — not merely once the camera stops hunting. If sharpness drops later, the app refocuses on its own. And a frame that isn't sharp never makes it into the work.

Two capture modes: On a tripod focus stays locked, which gives the calmest frames. Handheld it keeps tracking, because the distance to your work changes constantly. The motion sensor decides which applies; a tap on the viewfinder switches it.

Calmer display: The status in the viewfinder no longer flickers between states.
