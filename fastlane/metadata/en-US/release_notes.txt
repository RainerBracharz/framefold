FrameFold 2.6

Now on iPad too – and with sound.

• Full resolution: every frame comes as a photo from the sensor, export up to 4K. Plus lens choice (ultra wide, telephoto, front camera), clap to shoot, adding photos as frames and looks for the whole film.
• iPad: FrameFold runs on iPad, portrait and landscape. In a stand, viewfinder and frames follow the way the device is turned.
• Green screen, title and credits: replace green or blue with an image or a movie, plus a title card before the film and credits after it.
• Studio on iPad: from Advanced mode the camera becomes a pro workspace – timeline, toggle between live view and captured frames, multi-frame onion skin, masks, loupe, multiple audio tracks with waveform, X-sheet with mouth shapes.
• Record voice: the film plays along while you speak – the recording lands exactly in the right place. It stays on the device.
• Music or sound: pick an audio file in the work view – it plays under the exported film.
• Up to 30 fps: 15, 24, 25 and 30 fps for smooth motion.
• Manual camera (from Advanced): set ISO, shutter speed, focus and white balance yourself – the automatic metering then stays out of it.
• Duplicate and reorder frames: press and hold a frame for “Duplicate”, “Move earlier” and “Move later” – or simply drag it to a new place in the contact sheet.
• Now also in Spanish, Italian and Dutch – and a sample work is waiting on first launch to try things out.
