FrameFold 2.4

Steadier films and a new way to share.

• Reduce flicker: when exporting, FrameFold evens out brightness from frame to frame – clouds, room light or auto exposure no longer make the film flicker. Slow changes in light stay. On by default, can be turned off in the export settings.
• Share as GIF: below every finished film – an endlessly looping GIF for messengers, school platforms and slides that don’t play video.
