FrameFold 2.3.1

Smoother capture, and videos and live sessions together in one work.

• Add a video to a work: "Save" asks whether to start a new work or append the frames to an existing one.
• "Choose frames again" now in Simple mode too – drop a frame without analysing everything again.
• Undo: its own button in Simple mode; afterwards the onion skin shows the previous frame again.
• Capture stays smooth when the shutter fires; the hand check runs in the background.
• Rename and delete works with a long press; the contact sheet loads faster.
• Capture settings are remembered; the film loop in the viewfinder plays at the finished film's speed.
• Better VoiceOver support; dates in PDFs follow your language.
