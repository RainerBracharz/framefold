FrameFold 2.7

Now in 17 languages – and with tools like a pro studio.

• Camera moves: pan and zoom across part of the image, smooth or steady.
• Image layers: place your own images, also with transparency, over the film – in any order.
• Rotoscoping: bring in a video as frames, draw over it and replace the photo in the film with paper.
• Work as a file: long-press a work in the list → “Share as file …”. Via AirDrop or Files it goes to iPad, iPhone or Mac, where FrameFold opens a copy. No cloud, no account.
• Sound: five music pieces, voice effects (high, deep, robot), trim and fade audio tracks.
• Hold a frame: let a frame stay for 2, 3, 4 or 6 frames.
• Studio: lights via DMX (Art-Net) and motion control via Bluetooth – values per frame, set before each shot. Both only when you turn them on.
• New in Czech, Danish, Korean, Polish, Russian, Swedish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Chinese.
• Faster and safer: smoother Studio with many frames, faster export, checked work files.
