FrameFold 2.5

When the tripod gets bumped – and when one frame isn't right.

• Tripod moved? If the background sits differently than in the first frame, FrameFold tells you and can show the first frame to line things up again. “It's fine” keeps the new position.
• Retake a single frame: press and hold a frame in the contact sheet and choose “Retake” – the next frame replaces exactly this one, in place. The old one stays in the trash for 30 days.
• Fixed: “Continue with the camera” could get stuck on the result of the last session.
