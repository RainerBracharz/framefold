FrameFold 2.3

Tidied up and fine-tuned.

• "Works" and "Studio": the tab is now called Works, the third mode Studio.
• Continue with the camera: from any work straight back to the viewfinder; the camera can now list all your works.
• Switching tabs during a session finishes it instead of resetting it.
• Apple Watch: the shutter is only active while the iPhone viewfinder is open, and confirms every frame.
• Transitions stay steady with shake correction; frames of different sizes fill the frame.
• Flip book and exhibition reel in the app's colours; the flip book needs far less memory.
• Long videos can be cancelled; easier-to-read counter, VoiceOver announcements when a frame is taken.
