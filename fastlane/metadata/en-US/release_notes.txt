Letter, Dog face and Cup are available as fold sequences. For each step, the viewfinder shows the outline, the crease line and an arrow.
With “Auto shutter (hands away)”, the camera takes the frame as soon as the step is folded. A bar shows how far along you are.
Siri and the Shortcuts app know five commands, such as “Take a frame in FrameFold” and “Export stop-motion in FrameFold”.
On iPhone, the film preview in a work fills the width in portrait.
Help has two new chapters, “Fold sequences” and “Siri and Shortcuts”.
