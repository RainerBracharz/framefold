FrameFold 2.2

More for iPhone Duo.

• Film on the outer display: with the Duo unfolded and a session running, the outside shows the film so far, the frame count and what the camera is doing. You can turn it off in the capture settings.
• Viewfinder around the fold: unfolded in landscape, the camera and the film sit left and right of the fold – flat or half open.
• Folded shut, the camera image now runs all the way under the side bar.
• Two labels in the viewfinder now appear in English and French, too.
