FrameFold 2.1

The auto-shutter on a tripod has been reworked from the ground up.

• Slow hands: the camera now waits until your fingertips have left the frame, too.
• Low light and flickering lamps: your moves are detected reliably, no more missing frames.
• No duplicate frames: a reach that changes nothing, or a passing shadow, no longer fires the shutter.
• Focus: the camera now tells a plainer subject from a real loss of focus. It fires right away for the first and refocuses for the second.
