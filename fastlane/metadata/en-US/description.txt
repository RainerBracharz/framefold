The stop-motion app that shoots automatically as soon as your hands are out of the frame. Free, no subscription, no ads, no account and no internet.

ALL FREE, NO WATERMARKS
All features are free, including sound, music, green screen, titles, drawing and export up to 4K. No film has a watermark. FrameFold collects no data. Images, sound and works stay on your device.

AUTOMATIC CAMERA
Put your iPhone or iPad on a tripod and work. FrameFold shoots as soon as your hands are out of the frame and the scene is still. Exposure and white balance remain locked, the shutter speed locks against the flickering of artificial light. There is also onion skin, a warning for a moved camera, an interval shutter and a level.

SHARP IMAGES
The app constantly measures the sharpness and only locks the focus when it is right. A blurry image does not get into the work. From "Advanced" on, you set ISO, shutter speed, focus and white balance yourself and choose the lens. Every image has the full sensor resolution.

SHOOT WITHOUT TOUCHING
The Apple Watch becomes a remote shutter with a frame counter. Shooting also works with the volume buttons, with AirPods, by clapping or with the self-timer via a long press. Nobody has to touch the tripod.

THE FILM GROWS WITH YOU
From the second image on, the film runs as a loop in the viewfinder. A connected screen or projector shows the camera image without controls.

EDITING
Duplicate images, hold them, rearrange them by dragging or reshoot them individually. Everything can be undone. Text, speech bubbles and drawings go directly into the image, you put your own images as layers on top. Green screen replaces green or blue with an image or a film. From "Advanced" on, there are camera movements with pan and zoom. For rotoscoping, a video turns into images you draw over. There are also title cards, credits and looks for the whole film.

SOUND
Multiple audio tracks with waveforms. Music comes from your files or from five included tracks from "Happy" to "Dream". You attach ten sounds from Pop to Ta-da to any image. You record your voice to the running film, high, low or as a robot if you want. A voice on the device speaks typed text.

STUDIO
From "Advanced" on, the iPad becomes a studio workspace, the iPhone too if you want. With a timeline, switching between live image and recording, onion skin over multiple images, format masks, magnifying glass, reference image with chroma key and an X-Sheet for mouth shapes. You set light via DMX (Art-Net) and motion control via Bluetooth per image.

EXPORT AND FLIPBOOK
Export with 2 to 30 frames per second, up to 4K, as a loop, boomerang or backwards, also as a GIF. Every work becomes a print-ready flipbook if you want, with A4 sheets, cut lines and numbered pages. A work goes as a file via AirDrop to another device.

VIDEO TO STOP-MOTION
Choose an existing video. FrameFold detects the quiet moments, removes images with hands and sorts out duplicates.

FOR SCHOOLS
The "Simple" mode shows only the essentials. There is no account and no connection to the internet. A lesson plan for two hours is freely available, a short help is in the app.

ALSO ON THE MAC
The same app runs on the Mac, with a document camera or iPhone as a camera and export as ProRes or image sequence. FrameFold is available in 17 languages.

FrameFold was created as a tool for the artist Aldo Tolino. He folds printed photographs into objects and photographs them again.
