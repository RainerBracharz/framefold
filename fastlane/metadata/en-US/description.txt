The stop-motion app that shoots by itself the moment your hands leave the frame. Free, no subscription, no ads, no account, no internet.

EVERYTHING FREE, NO WATERMARK
Sound, sound effects, text to speech, green screen, titles and end credits, text and speech bubbles, drawing, photo import, export up to 4K — none of it costs extra, and no film ever carries a watermark. Nothing leaves your device: no cloud, no account, no tracking.

AUTOMATIC CAMERA
iPhone or iPad on a tripod, then work — FrameFold fires by itself as soon as your hands leave the frame and the scene settles. Exposure and white balance are locked, and the shutter speed locks in to stop artificial light from flickering. With onion skin, a warning if the camera has shifted, an interval trigger and a spirit level. Every frame at full sensor resolution, with your choice of lens.

SHARP, OR NOT AT ALL
The app measures image sharpness continuously and only locks focus once it has really settled. A frame that isn't sharp never makes it into the work. From Advanced mode you can also set ISO, shutter speed, focus and white balance yourself.

SHOOT WITHOUT TOUCHING
Apple Watch as a remote with a frame counter, the volume buttons, AirPods, a clap, or a countdown with a long press. Nobody has to bump the tripod.

STUDIO
From Advanced mode the iPad becomes a pro workspace — and the iPhone too, if you like: timeline, toggle between live view and captured frames, multi-frame onion skin, aspect masks, loupe, a reference image with chroma key and an X-Sheet with mouth shapes.

SOUND
Multiple audio tracks with waveform: music from your files, record your voice while the film plays, attach ten sound effects from Pop to Ta-da to any frame, or have a voice speak the text you type.

TEXT, TITLES, GREEN SCREEN
Speech bubbles, text and drawings right in the frame. Title card and end credits. Replace green or blue with an image or a film. Looks for the whole film.

EDIT AND EXPORT
Duplicate frames, drag them into a new order, retake single frames, undo anything. Export at 2 to 30 fps, up to 4K, as a loop, boomerang or in reverse, and as a GIF too.

FLIP BOOK
Every work becomes a print-ready flip book: A4 sheets with cut lines and numbered leaves. Cut, staple, flip.

FOR SCHOOLS
Simple mode shows only the essentials. No account, no data processing, no internet — it fits any school's data-protection rules. A lesson plan for two lessons is freely available, and a short help guide is built into the app.

VIDEO TO STOP-MOTION
Pick an existing video: the calm moments get found, hands removed, duplicates sorted out.

ON THE MAC TOO
The same app runs on the Mac, with a document camera or an iPhone as the camera, and exports ProRes or an image sequence. In 17 languages.

Built as a bespoke tool for the artist Aldo Tolino, whose work folds printed photographs into objects and photographs them again — image, object, image.
