The stop-motion app for the Mac that shoots automatically as soon as your hands are out of the frame. Free, no subscription, no ads, no account and no internet.

ALL FREE, NO WATERMARKS
All features are free, including sound, music, green screen, titles, drawing and export up to 4K or as ProRes. No film has a watermark. FrameFold collects no data. Images, sound and works stay on your Mac.

CAMERA ABOVE THE TABLE
A camera looks down at the table, you fold, draw or place things. FrameFold shoots as soon as your hands are out of the frame and the scene is still. This works best with a document camera, FrameFold selects a plugged-in USB camera on its own. An iPhone as a camera or the built-in camera work too. The space bar shoots manually. There is also onion skin, a warning for a moved camera and an interval shutter.

SHARP IMAGES
The app constantly measures the sharpness. A blurry image does not get into the work. Where the camera allows it, exposure and white balance remain locked, so nothing flickers between images.

DSLR AND MIRRORLESS
Via a watched folder, every photo from your camera gets into the work in full resolution, even RAW.

STUDIO
From "Advanced" on, the camera becomes a studio workspace. With a timeline, switching between live image and recording, onion skin over multiple images, difference and exposure view, format masks, safe areas, grids, reference image or film with chroma key, notes per image, tools against noise, dimming when shooting, test shots and time-lapse with start and end time. All commands also work via the keyboard. You set light via DMX (Art-Net) and motion control via Bluetooth per image.

THE FILM GROWS WITH YOU
While you work, the stop-motion already runs as a loop next to the camera image.

EDITING
Duplicate images, hold them, rearrange them by dragging or reshoot them individually. Everything can be undone. Text, speech bubbles and drawings go directly into the image, you put your own images as layers on top. Green screen replaces green or blue with an image or a film. From "Advanced" on, there are camera movements with pan and zoom. For rotoscoping, a video turns into images you draw over. There are also title cards, credits and looks for the whole film.

SOUND
Multiple audio tracks with waveforms. Music comes from your files or from five included tracks from "Happy" to "Dream". You attach ten sounds from Pop to Ta-da to any image. You record your voice to the running film, high, low or as a robot if you want. A voice on the Mac speaks typed text. In the X-Sheet you assign mouth shapes to the images.

EXPORT AND FLIPBOOK
Export with 2 to 30 frames per second, up to 4K, as ProRes or as an image sequence. Every work becomes a print-ready flipbook if you want, with A4 sheets, cut lines and numbered pages.

VIDEO TO STOP-MOTION
Choose an existing video. FrameFold detects the quiet moments, removes images with hands and sorts out duplicates. Beforehand, you see every image on the contact sheet.

FOR SCHOOLS
The "Simple" mode shows only the essentials. There is no account and no connection to the internet. A lesson plan for two hours is freely available, a short help is in the app.

ALSO ON IPHONE AND IPAD
FrameFold is also available for iPhone and iPad, there with a tripod, Apple Watch as a remote shutter, and shooting by clapping. FrameFold is available in 17 languages.

FrameFold was created as a tool for the artist Aldo Tolino. He folds printed photographs into objects and photographs them again.
