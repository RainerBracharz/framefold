The stop-motion app that shoots by itself the moment your hands leave the frame. Free, no subscription, no ads, no account, no internet.

FrameFold turns your work at the table into a stop-motion — automatically, and entirely on your Mac.

A camera looks down at the table while you fold, draw, arrange. FrameFold fires by itself as soon as your hands leave the frame and the scene settles. Nothing leaves your Mac: no cloud, no account, no tracking.

CAMERA ABOVE THE TABLE
Works best with a document camera looking straight down at the table — FrameFold picks a connected USB camera on its own. An iPhone as a webcam or the built-in camera can be chosen too. With onion skin, drift check and an interval trigger; the space bar fires by hand.

SHARP, OR NOT AT ALL
The app measures image sharpness continuously. A frame that isn't sharp never makes it into the work. Where the camera allows, FrameFold holds exposure and white balance so nothing flickers between frames.

STUDIO
From Advanced mode the camera becomes a pro workspace: timeline, toggle between live view and captured frames, multi-frame onion skin, difference and exposure views, aspect masks, safe areas, grids, a reference image or movie with chroma key, notes per frame, frame averaging against noise, screen blackout on capture, test shots and time-lapse with start and end time. All on the keyboard too. Export as ProRes or an image sequence.

EDIT AND SCORE
Duplicate a frame to hold a pose longer, drag frames into a new order, export at up to 24 fps — and lay music or sound from your files under the film.

THE FILM GROWS AS YOU WORK
While you work, the stop-motion is already playing as a loop next to the camera feed.

FLIP BOOK
Every work becomes a print-ready flip book: A4 sheets with cut lines, a binding edge and numbered leaves. Cut, staple, flip — the film becomes an object again.

VIDEO TO STOP-MOTION
Pick an existing video, the calm moments get found, hands removed, duplicates sorted out. You check every frame on the contact sheet before it's assembled.

THREE MODES
Simple (pick, shoot, done — made for teaching), Advanced (aspect, frame rate, resolution, playback) and a studio mode with paper relief, image echo, print look, fold transitions, an exhibition reel and print-ready contact-sheet and fold-template PDFs.

WORKS, NOT FILES
Projects collect frames across any number of sessions, with a 30-day trash. The result arrives like an edition: "Sheets 01–24 · Series of 24 · dated 2026".

ON IPHONE AND IPAD TOO
FrameFold is also available for iPhone and iPad — there with a tripod, Apple Watch as a remote and a studio monitor.

Built as a bespoke tool for the artist Aldo Tolino, whose work folds printed photographs into objects and photographs them again — image, object, image.
