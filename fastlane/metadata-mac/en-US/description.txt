The stop-motion app for the Mac that takes the shot by itself as soon as your hands leave the frame. Free, with no subscription, no ads, no account and no internet.

EVERYTHING FREE, NO WATERMARKS
Every feature is free, including sound, music, green screen, titles, drawing and export up to 4K or as ProRes. No film ever carries a watermark. FrameFold collects no data. Your frames, sound and works stay on your Mac.

CAMERA ABOVE THE TABLE
A camera looks down at the table while you fold, draw or move things around. FrameFold shoots as soon as your hands leave the frame and the scene is still. A document camera works best, and FrameFold picks a plugged-in USB camera automatically. You can also use an iPhone or the built-in camera. Press the space bar to shoot by hand. You also get onion skin, a warning when the camera has moved and an interval timer.

SHARP FRAMES
The app measures sharpness continuously. A blurry frame never makes it into your work. Where the camera allows it, exposure and white balance stay locked so nothing flickers between frames.

DSLR AND MIRRORLESS
Through a watched folder, every photo from your camera lands in your work at full resolution, RAW included.

STUDIO
From “Advanced” mode on, the camera view turns into a studio workspace. You get a timeline, a toggle between live view and captured frames, multi-frame onion skin, difference and exposure views, aspect masks, safe areas, grids, a reference image or video with chroma key, notes per frame, frame averaging against noise, blackout while shooting, test shots and time-lapse with start and end times. Every command has a keyboard shortcut. Lights via DMX (Art-Net) and motion control via Bluetooth are set per frame.

WATCH THE FILM GROW
While you work, your stop-motion already plays as a loop next to the camera feed.

EDITING
Duplicate, hold, reorder or reshoot any frame. Everything can be undone. Add text, speech bubbles and drawings right on the frame, and lay your own images on top as layers. Green screen swaps green or blue for an image or a video. From “Advanced” mode on, you get camera moves with pan and zoom. For rotoscoping, a video becomes frames you can draw over. Title cards, credits and looks for the whole film are included too.

SOUND
Multiple audio tracks with waveforms. Use music from your files or one of five built-in tracks, from “Happy” to “Dream”. Attach any of ten sound effects, from Pop to Ta-da, to any frame. Record your voice while the film plays, and make it high, low or robotic if you like. An on-device voice reads out text you type. In the X-Sheet you match mouth shapes to frames.

EXPORT AND FLIP BOOK
Export at 2 to 30 frames per second, up to 4K, as ProRes or as an image sequence. Any work can become a print-ready flip book with A4 sheets, cut lines and numbered pages.

VIDEO TO STOP-MOTION
Pick an existing video. FrameFold finds the still moments, removes frames with hands in them and drops duplicates. You check every frame on the contact sheet first.

FOR SCHOOLS
“Simple” mode shows only what you need. There is no account and no internet connection. A free two-lesson plan is available, and a short guide is built into the app.

ALSO ON IPHONE AND IPAD
FrameFold is also available for iPhone and iPad, with tripod shooting, Apple Watch as a remote and clap to shoot. FrameFold is available in 17 languages.

FrameFold began as a tool for the artist Aldo Tolino, who folds printed photographs into objects and photographs them again.
