FrameFold 2.3 for Mac

• Menus and shortcuts: ⌘N new work, ⌘O open video, ⌘↩ done, ⌘⌫ undo last frame, ⌘1–3 for the sections.
• Open videos straight from the Finder or drag them onto the window.
• Save or print flip books and films right from the share menu.
• Plug in a camera whenever you like: FrameFold picks it up on its own and switches to the next one if it's unplugged.
• Your Mac stays awake during a session.
• New layout: camera and film side by side, controls below.
• Plus everything from the iPhone version: "Works" and "Studio", steadier transitions, exhibition reel in the app's colours.
