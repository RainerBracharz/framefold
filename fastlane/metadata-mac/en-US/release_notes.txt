FrameFold 2.7

Take your works with you – and sound for kids' films.

• Work as a file: right-click a work in the list → “Share as file …”. Via AirDrop or Files it goes to iPad, iPhone or Mac, where FrameFold opens a copy. No cloud, no account.
• Music: five pieces from “Happy” to “Chase”, as long as your film, fading out at the end.
• Voice effects: high voice, deep voice or robot for any audio track.
• Trim audio tracks and fade them in or out.
• Hold a frame: a frame can stay for 2, 3, 4 or 6 frames – one step, one undo.
• The “Atelier” mode now has that name in every language.
