FrameFold 2.6

The Studio – and sound.

• Studio: from Advanced mode the camera becomes a pro workspace – draggable timeline, toggle between live view and captured frames, multi-frame onion skin, difference and exposure views, aspect masks, grids, a reference image or movie with chroma key, notes per frame, frame averaging against noise, time-lapse with start and end time. All on the keyboard too (Studio menu).
• Any camera: photos your DSLR or mirrorless camera’s software drops into a folder become the next frame at full resolution – RAW too. Plus a loupe for checking focus in the live view.
• Sound like a studio: multiple audio tracks with start frame and level, waveform in the timeline, sound while playing and stepping. Read dialogue in the X-sheet with mouth shapes per character – FrameFold can recognise the words itself, on the device only.
• Export as ProRes or an image sequence (PNG, TIFF, JPEG).
• Music or sound: pick an audio file in the work view – it plays under the exported film.
• Up to 24 fps: 15 and 24 fps for smooth motion.
• Duplicate and reorder frames: right-click a frame for “Duplicate”, “Move earlier” and “Move later” – or drag it to a new place in the contact sheet.
