The fold template offers hypar, circular pleat and Miura-ori with mountain and valley folds. Hypar and circular pleat follow Origami Simulator by Amanda Ghassaei, made with Erik Demaine and Tomohiro Tachi.
The crease pattern can run as a guide in the viewfinder. It lies on the flat sheet and shows the next fold with every frame.
The result after “Done” in the Camera tab has the work's sound tracks.
Text, drawings and camera moves stay with their frames when you remove, duplicate, hold or reorder frames.
“Restore” puts frames from the trash back in their old place.
“Undo” also covers title, credits, name and dialogue.
Speech output offers voices in all 17 languages of the app.
Playback in the studio stays in sync with the sound.
