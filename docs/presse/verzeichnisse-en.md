## Short description (about 150 characters)

Free stop-motion app that shoots by itself when hands leave the frame. No account, no ads, no internet connection, everything stays on the device.

## Long description

Students build a story from paper, clay or toy figures and film it frame by frame. The device stays on a stand: FrameFold detects hands and takes the picture as soon as they leave the frame and the scene is still. Nobody has to touch the device while filming, so groups can focus on the story.

Simple mode shows only the essentials. Advanced tools include onion skin, multiple audio tracks, voice recording, sound effects, text to speech, green screen, titles, speech bubbles, drawing and export up to 4K. All features are free, with no watermark.

## For schools

- No student accounts and no sign-in
- No ads, no tracking, no data collected by the developer
- Photos, sound and films stay on the device unless a user shares them
- The app connects to the internet at no point. Two optional studio features for professional sets connect only when switched on: lighting control on the local network and motion control via Bluetooth
- A two-lesson plan is available under CC BY 4.0 (German): https://github.com/RainerBracharz/framefold/tree/main/docs/unterricht
- Privacy fact sheet for school administrators (German): https://github.com/RainerBracharz/framefold/tree/main/docs/schule

## Learning goals

- Plan a story and turn it into a film
- Understand how motion arises from single images
- Work in teams with changing roles (director, set, control)
- Subjects: art, language (storytelling), science (motion, time), computing and media education
